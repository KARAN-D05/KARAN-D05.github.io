Building computation from first principles.

I build systems from first principles to understand how structure gives rise to computation, and how computation gives rise to intelligence.

"Dovere is my hand, tho small,
May I not add with truth;
I do my best to please you all,
Encourage then my youth." The Maillardet Automaton, c. 1800

My work explores intelligence, thought, language, and machinery through the lens of computation - investigating how simple logical structures combine to form memory, control, adaptation, and learning.

Projects

Seven systems, one continuous line of inquiry, from feedback-driven arithmetic to hardware that adapts. Each was carried past simulation into synthesis: gate-level netlists, static timing analysis, and power estimation on real standard-cell libraries.

Harvard Processor

View →

A parameterized multi-cycle Harvard-architecture processor built from 12 RTL modules, with an Amdahl's Law-backed study of a control-path optimization.

Verilog, cocotb, Sky130HD, Amdahl's Law

High-Performance Adder Architectures

View →

Six 64-bit adder topologies characterized on identical terms, from Ripple-Carry to Kogge-Stone, mapping the area/speed/power trade-off in carry computation.

Verilog, Sky130HD, Nangate45, PPA analysis

High-Performance Multipliers

View →

Four 32-bit multiplier architectures compared by latency and throughput rather than clock speed alone, including the effect of the final adder stage.

Verilog, Sky130HD, throughput analysis

Gate-Level Cybernetic Classifier

View →

A binary classifier that corrects its own decision boundary from feedback in pure deterministic hardware, with two algorithms and formal convergence proofs.

Verilog, Logisim, Sky130HD

Repeated Arithmetic Machine

View →

A 4-bit feedback-driven machine that evolves across five versions, from a single self-correcting adder to a stored-program automaton executing instructions from memory.

Verilog, Logisim, Yosys, OpenSTA

Assembler

View →

A custom assembly language and Python assembler for the machine above, with lexical, syntax, and semantic analysis built up across three versions.

Python, custom ISA, lexer/parser

Artificial Neuron

View →

A gate-level McCulloch–Pitts neuron in Verilog, reproducing AND, OR, and NOT through threshold and inhibitory-weight adjustment alone.

Verilog, Yosys, GTKWave
